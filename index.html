CHAPTER 00 — KOZHIKODE

A cup that
stayed with us

Bermito began with an unexpected cup of nano-lot coffee. It was strong, vibrant and impossible to recreate. What made it special was also what made it momentary.

That cup changed the way we looked at coffee.

We began Bermito with a belief that rarity is not a flaw. It is the point.

The Bermito story in five chapters

    CHAPTER 01

    THE CUP THAT STAYED

    It began with a cup that could never be repeated. A small nano-lot, full of life, character and a moment that would never arrive in exactly the same way again.

    That cup became the beginning of Bermito.

    A FIRST CUPPING

    CHAPTER 02

    BORN IN KOZHIKODE

    In Kozhikode, a city shaped by trade, hospitality and flavour, we began roasting coffee differently.

    Slowly. Carefully. One small batch at a time.

    KOZHIKODE / KERALA

    CHAPTER 03

    GOING WHERE COFFEE TAKES US

    We go wherever coffee takes us — farms, mountains and quiet corners where it grows at its own pace.

    We look for curious producers, thoughtful processes and coffees with something honest to say.

    AT ORIGIN

    CHAPTER 04

    ROASTED WITH INTENTION

    We roast with intention, not urgency.

    Every batch is handled with patience, listening to the coffee and responding to it rather than forcing a formula.

    Some coffees last for weeks. Some never return. That is not a flaw. That is the point.

    IN THE ROASTERY

    CHAPTER 05

    LIVE THROUGH COFFEE

    Bermito is for those who believe coffee is more than a habit.

    It is a moment. A ritual. A quiet companion through the day.

    We live through coffee.

    THE BREW ROOM

    THE ART OF TASTING

    More than a logo.
    An experience.

    Inspired by the ritual of tasting, the Bermito mark follows the journey from observation to the final sip. It is a symbol of appreciation for those who choose to notice what is inside the cup.

    OBSERVE

    Before taking a sip, notice its colour, clarity and movement in the cup.

    SMELL

    Inhale the aroma before sipping — floral, nutty, chocolaty?

    SIP & SLURP

    Take a small sip, let it coat your tongue, and slurp gently to spread the flavours.

    ENJOY & FINISH

    Notice how the flavours evolve and the aftertaste lingers.

    What we hold to

    CLARITY

    We roast and communicate with honesty, allowing each coffee to show its own character.

    CONTINUITY

    We respect the relationships, knowledge and rituals that carry coffee from the land to the cup.

    RESPONSIBILITY

    We care for the coffee, the people and the work behind every batch.
